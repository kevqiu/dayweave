The day's weather, in a badge beside the day

Three ways to put the forecast on each day header in the sheet under the map. Each badge holds the same three things, in the order you asked for. The phones show the same trip on the same morning: Japan, autumn, day 3 of 11, with Fri Oct 2 open.

The same in all three

No badge is better than a made-up badge. A day with no forecast yet (further out than the forecast reaches, about 10 to 16 days) or with no located stop to take a forecast for shows nothing. That follows the nothing is a placeholder rule.

The forecast is taken at the day's centroid, the same point the trip card's map strip already computes. The temperature is the day's high.

Other screens

Whichever you pick, the same badge goes on the desktop itinerary rail's day headers. On the Planner I'd put it in the column header after the city and the count.

The pin colours on the map don't change. Weather stays in the list.

Questions for you

Past days: show what the weather was, or nothing?

The mockups show it for Sep 30 and Oct 1. It's useful as a trip record, but it takes space on rows you no longer act on.

Data source: Open-Meteo or Google Weather?

Open-Meteo needs no key and forecasts 16 days ahead. Google's Weather API uses the Maps key you already have, forecasts 10 days ahead, and is billed per call.
